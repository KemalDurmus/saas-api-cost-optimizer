Find the waste in your SaaS & API bills

Paste or upload a cost export and get a breakdown by vendor, month-over-month growth, spikes, possible double charges, fixed subscriptions and unit-price increases — in seconds.

What it accepts

Any CSV with a service/vendor and a cost/amount column. Date and usage/quantity are optional but unlock trends and unit-price checks. Comma, semicolon or tab separated; amounts like $1,250.00 or 1.250,50 both work.

Private by design

Your file is analyzed in memory and never stored. No account needed.
